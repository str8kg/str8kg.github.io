Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What the app is

PrimeView Clip is a desktop app that runs on the owner's own computer. It creates short clips from podcast videos and publishes them to the owner's connected social media accounts.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic account info (open ID, display name, avatar): used to show which account a video will be posted to.
  • Access tokens: used to upload videos to the connected account.
  • Post status and IDs: used to confirm that a video was published and to link to it.

How the data is stored

All data is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Tokens are used only to call the official APIs of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account is connected. Disconnecting the app on TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) revokes its access, and the stored tokens are deleted from the computer. You can also ask for deletion by email.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.
