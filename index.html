PrimeView Clip

PodcastClipper — desktop app

PrimeView Clip is a desktop app that turns long podcast episodes into short vertical clips and publishes them to our own social media accounts: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

How it works

  1. We choose a podcast episode and the app suggests the best moments.
  2. The app cuts each moment into a vertical clip with captions and b-roll.
  3. We review every clip in the app. Nothing is published until we approve it.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to our connected accounts, including TikTok through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

TikTok integration

The app uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to upload approved videos with their caption. Before posting, it shows which account the video will go to and the privacy and interaction settings that will be used.
